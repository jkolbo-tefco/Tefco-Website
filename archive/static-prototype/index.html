Questions

FAQs

What custom fabrication services do you provide?+
Our experienced fabricators have over a decade of sheet metal fabrication expertise (aluminum, corten, stainless, and steel). Need TIG, MIG or stick welding? Our team will perform both decorative and structural designs.
What metals do you work with?+
Alloy steel, aluminum, carbon steel, copper, copper alloys, galvanized steel, iron, nickel, silicon bronze, stainless steel, steel, tool steel. If you don't know which metal will give you the look you want, our experts can guide you to the right choice.
What custom made projects can you assist with?+
Our custom fabrication is nearly limitless. From your home to your retail store or office we can help you design and create just about anything. Here are some ideas that we can help you brainstorm: Edging, Fire Pits, Ladders, Radius Containers, Railings, Roof Racks, Table Frames, Tool Boxes, Planters — Railings and more!
