How It Works

Our Powder Coating Process

Every piece that leaves our shop goes through the same six steps — no shortcuts, no skipped stages, no exceptions. We treat surface prep as seriously as the finish itself, because the best powder in the world won't last on a poorly prepared substrate. That's why we handle sandblasting, degreasing, and inspection in-house: we control every variable that determines how long the coating lasts.

Owning the entire process under one roof also means faster turnaround. There's no waiting on a sub-contractor to return your part, no coordination delays, no surprises. When we quote a lead time, we hit it — and the finish you get on day one looks the same in year ten.

01
Step One

Inspect & Assess

We evaluate the part — material, existing coatings, condition, and end-use environment. This determines the right prep method, primer if needed, and ideal powder formulation.

02
Step Two

Strip & Degrease

Old paint, oil, mill scale, and contaminants are removed. We degrease chemically first — a step many shops skip that causes adhesion failures and early peeling down the road.

03
Step Three

In-House Sand / Shot Blast

Every piece is blasted to a near-white metal profile in our own facility. This creates a mechanical anchor for the powder and ensures the finish bonds at the molecular level.

04
Step Four

Electrostatic Application

Dry powder is electrostatically charged as it's sprayed, clinging uniformly to the grounded metal. Even coverage with no runs, sags, or thin spots — even on complex shapes.

05
Step Five

Oven Cure

Parts are cured at high temperature, causing the powder to melt, flow, and cross-link into a hard, continuous film. The result outlasts paint by years and shrugs off UV, freeze-thaw cycles, and road salt.

06
Step Six

Inspect & Release

Every finished piece is visually inspected for coverage, color consistency, and surface quality before it leaves the shop. Doesn't meet our standard? It goes back through.
