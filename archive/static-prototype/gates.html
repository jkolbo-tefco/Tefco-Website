Questions

Gate FAQs

What types of gates do you build?+
We specialize in all types of custom iron gates, including entry gates, driveway gates, garden gates, and security gates — each designed around your property and built to order.
Do you install the gates you build?+
Yes. We measure your opening on site, fabricate the gate in our Ogden shop, and our own crew handles installation for a proper, lasting fit.
How are your gates finished?+
Every gate is finished with our in-house powder coating — hundreds of colors and a hard, weather-resistant finish that holds up to Utah seasons without chipping or fading.
