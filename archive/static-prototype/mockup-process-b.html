How It Works

Our Powder Coating Process

Every piece goes through the same six steps — no shortcuts. Prep is everything, and we do it all in-house.

1
Step One
01

Inspect & Assess

We evaluate the part — material, existing coatings, condition, and the end-use environment. This determines the right prep method, primer if needed, and the ideal powder formulation for your project.

Step Two
02

Strip & Degrease

Old paint, oil, mill scale, and contaminants are removed. We degrease chemically first — a step many shops skip that causes adhesion failures and early peeling down the road.

2
3
Step Three
03

In-House Sand / Shot Blast

Every piece is blasted to a near-white metal profile in our own facility. This creates a mechanical anchor, removes all rust and scale, and ensures the finish bonds at the molecular level — not just the surface.

Step Four
04

Electrostatic Application

Dry powder is electrostatically charged as it's sprayed, clinging uniformly to the grounded metal. The result is an even, consistent coat with no runs, sags, or thin spots — even on complex shapes.

4
5
Step Five
05

Oven Cure

Parts are cured in our oven at high temperature, causing the powder to melt, flow, and cross-link into a hard, continuous film. The result outlasts paint by years and shrugs off UV, moisture, and impact.

Step Six
06

Inspect & Release

Every finished piece is visually inspected for coverage, color consistency, and surface quality. If it doesn't meet our standard, it goes back through. That's the satisfaction guarantee.
