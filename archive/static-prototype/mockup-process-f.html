How It Works

Our Powder Coating Process

Every piece goes through the same six steps — no shortcuts. Prep is everything, and we do it all in-house.

01
Step One
Inspect & Assess

We evaluate the part — material, existing coatings, condition, and the end-use environment. This determines the right prep method, primer if needed, and the ideal powder formulation for your project.

02
Step Two
Strip & Degrease

Old paint, oil, mill scale, and contaminants are removed. We degrease chemically first — a step many shops skip that leads to adhesion failures and early peeling down the road.

03
Step Three
In-House Sand / Shot Blast

Every piece is blasted to a near-white metal profile in our own facility. This creates a mechanical anchor for the powder, removes all rust and scale, and ensures the finish bonds at the molecular level.

04
Step Four
Electrostatic Application

Dry powder is electrostatically charged as it's sprayed, clinging uniformly to the grounded metal. The result is an even, consistent coat with no runs, sags, or thin spots — even on complex shapes and recesses.

05
Step Five
Oven Cure

Parts are cured at high temperature, causing the powder to melt, flow, and cross-link into a hard, continuous film. The result outlasts paint by years and shrugs off Utah's UV, freeze-thaw cycles, and road salt.

06
Step Six
Inspect & Release

Every finished piece is visually inspected for coverage, color consistency, and surface quality before it leaves the shop. If it doesn't meet our standard, it goes back through — that's the satisfaction guarantee.
