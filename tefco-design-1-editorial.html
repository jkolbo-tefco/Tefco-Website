About Tefco

We build metalwork that outlasts trends — one-of-a-kind designs, fabricated and installed by our own crew.

TEFCO has served Weber County with high-rated ornamental iron products and services since 1942. Over the decades we've earned a reputation for quality craftsmanship and unique, one-of-a-kind designs.

We take pride in getting to know our customers and putting their needs first — from the first measurement to the final install.
