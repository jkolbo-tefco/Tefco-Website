# TEFCO — Content, SEO & Conversion Strategy

A plan to turn the website into a lead-generating sales channel: rank in Google
(especially local search), show up in AI answers (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews,
etc.), and convert visitors into estimate requests. Written for a non-SEO
audience — jargon is explained as we go.

> **Honest expectation-setting:** SEO is compounding, not instant. Local results
> can move in weeks; competitive organic rankings take months of consistent
> content + reviews + links. The good news: as a real, established local
> business (since 1942) with actual projects and reviews, TEFCO has the
> strongest foundation there is. Most competitors aren't doing this well.

---

## 1. SEO in plain English (the 5 ideas that matter here)

- **Keyword** = the phrase a person types/says. We want a clear primary phrase
  per page (e.g. "powder coating Ogden") plus related phrases.
- **Search intent** = what the searcher actually wants. "powder coating cost" =
  researching; "powder coating near me" = ready to buy. Match the page to intent.
- **The Local Pack / Map results** = the map + 3 businesses at the top of local
  searches. This is driven mostly by your **Google Business Profile** + reviews +
  consistent business info — NOT the website alone. For a local shop this is the
  #1 lever.
- **Topic clusters** = a main "hub" page (e.g. Railings) supported by deeper
  pages and blog posts (cable railing, stair railing, railing cost, railing
  maintenance) that all link to each other. Google rewards depth on a topic.
- **Schema (structured data)** = invisible code that tells search engines and AI
  exactly what you are (a LocalBusiness in Ogden), what you offer (Services),
  your reviews, your FAQs. We already add this on the new site — it directly
  helps both Google rich results and AI answers.

## 2. The three biggest levers (do these first)

1. **Google Business Profile (GBP) + reviews.** For "near me" and map searches
   this outranks everything. Fully optimize the profile, post weekly, and build
   a steady stream of reviews. (Playbook in §6.)
2. **Make every page genuinely unique and helpful.** The current site repeats
   the same testimonials, feature blocks, and even FAQs across pages — that's
   "thin/duplicate content" and it holds rankings back. Each page should answer
   its own buyer's questions with original copy, real photos, and a clear next step.
3. **Publish consistently (the content engine).** Weekly articles + video +
   social tell Google and AI that TEFCO is active and authoritative, and they
   create dozens of new "doors" into the site for long-tail searches. (§8.)

naturally for humans. Repeating it unnaturally hurts more than it helps.

## 4. Content & structure improvements (per page)

**Kill the duplication.** Give each service page its own:
- A specific, benefit-led intro (what they get + why TEFCO + that it's local).
- **Unique body sections:** the process, the materials/options, why it matters
  in Utah's climate, what affects cost, and what to expect.
- **2-3 relevant testimonials** (not the same 7 everywhere) — ideally ones that
  mention that service.
- **Topic-specific FAQs** with real answers (fixes the current gates=powder-coating
  / railings=sandblasting FAQ mismatch) — and they power FAQ schema + AI answers.
- **Internal links** to related services ("pairs well with our powder coating").

**Add these high-converting pages/sections the site lacks:**
- **Our Process** (Consult → Design → Fabricate → Finish → Install) — answers the
  #1 buyer anxiety ("what's it like to work with you?").
- **Pricing / "What affects the cost"** guidance — high-intent searchers love
  transparency; you don't need exact prices, just the factors and "free estimate."
- **Project case studies** (real jobs: problem → solution → result + photos) —
  these rank, build trust, and feed social/video.
- **Financing or warranty details** if applicable.
- **Service-area** content (the cities you cover) on the contact/home page.

**Make content scannable:** short paragraphs, bullet lists, clear headings,
comparison tables (e.g., metals for planters, railing styles) — humans skim, and
AI engines lift answers straight from well-structured lists/tables.

## 6. Local SEO playbook (the highest ROI work)

1. **Google Business Profile** — claim/verify; fill out everything:
   - Categories: primary "Metal Fabricator"; add "Welder", "Powder Coating
     Service", "Ironworks/Ornamental", "Metal Workshop" as applicable.
   - Services + descriptions, service-area cities, exact hours, website, phone.
   - Photos: add several weekly (projects, shop, team) — active profiles rank better.
   - Use **GBP Posts** weekly (repurpose your blog/social — see §8).
   - Fill the **Q&A** with your real FAQs.
2. **Reviews engine** — the single biggest local ranking + trust factor:
   - Ask every happy customer (text them the GBP review link the day after install).
   - Aim for a steady drip (e.g., 4-8/month) rather than bursts.
   - **Respond to every review**, working in natural phrases ("Thanks for letting
     us build your **iron railings in Ogden**!").
3. **NAP consistency** — your Name, Address, Phone must match **exactly**
   everywhere (website, GBP, Yelp, BBB, Houzz, Angi, Facebook, chamber, local
   directories). Inconsistency confuses Google and AI.
4. **Citations/listings** — get listed in the big directories + Utah/Weber County
   local ones. Quality and consistency over quantity.
5. **On-site local signals** — LocalBusiness schema (done), embedded map,
   city/area mentions in copy, and the consistent "Ogden & Weber County, Utah."

## 7. Getting into AI answers (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity)

AI answer engines favor sources that are **clear, factual, well-structured, and
corroborated across the web**. To get TEFCO recommended when someone asks an AI
"who does custom iron railings near Ogden?":

- **Answer questions directly and concisely** in the content (a one-sentence
  answer, then detail). AI lifts those.
- **Use Q&A, definitions, lists, and tables** — easy for models to extract.
- **Structured data everywhere** — LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, Product (for
  planters w/ dimensions), Review/AggregateRating. (We add these on the new site.)
- **Entity consistency** — same business name, location, and service language
  across the site, GBP, and directories so AI confidently associates
  "TEFCO = custom iron + powder coating in Ogden, UT."
- **Be the most complete source** on your niche topics (e.g., a genuinely
  thorough "powder coating in Utah" guide) — depth wins citations.
- **Earn mentions/links** from local news, suppliers, partners, Houzz, etc.
  Off-site reputation feeds AI's confidence.
- **Keep facts unambiguous** — fix the conflicting "since 1942 / 1973 / 73 / 75 /
  79 / 80 years" so every source agrees. (Use **1942 / 80+ years**.)

## 8. The weekly content engine (articles + video + social)

The efficient way: **one project or topic → many pieces.** Don't create from
scratch each channel; capture once, repurpose everywhere.

### Weekly rhythm (repurposing flow)
1. **Capture** one real job or topic (photos + a 60-90s phone video on site).
2. **1 blog article** (~600-1,000 words) targeting a long-tail keyword/question,
   ending with a CTA and linking to the related service page.
3. **1 YouTube video** (the clip, lightly edited) — YouTube is the #2 search
   engine and feeds Google; embed it in the blog post (+ VideoObject schema).
4. **3-5 social posts** (Instagram/Facebook reels + before/after + a "color of
   the week") pulled from the same shoot.
5. **1 Google Business Profile post** linking to the article.
This is ~one shoot + one writing session per week, fanned out to 5 channels.

### Article ideas (each targets a real search + supports a service page)
- "How much does powder coating cost in Utah? (2026 guide)"
- "Powder coating vs. paint: which lasts longer?"
- "How to keep wrought iron railings rust-free through a Utah winter"
- "Cable railing vs. glass vs. traditional: pros, cons, cost"
- "Are iron security doors worth it? Security, value, and style"
- "Corten vs. aluminum vs. steel planters: how to choose"
- "Do floating stairs need a wall? How we engineer them safely"
- "What is shot blasting, and when do you need it?"
- Project spotlights: "How we built a 16-ft driveway gate in North Ogden"
- Seasonal: "Spring deck railing refresh", "Powder coating patio furniture"

### Content types that convert + rank
How-to/maintenance · buyer & cost guides · material comparisons (great tables) ·
project case studies · behind-the-scenes shop/team · color & finish showcases ·
customer features.

### Why this hits every goal
- **SEO:** each post is a new ranking page for a specific question; internal
  links strengthen the money pages.
- **AI answers:** clear Q&A/how-to content is exactly what models cite.
- **Conversion:** builds trust and keeps you top-of-mind; every piece routes to a quote.
- **"Active business" signal:** fresh content + weekly GBP/social posts show
  customers (and algorithms) you're thriving.

- ◻️ Add `VideoObject` schema when embedding videos; `BreadcrumbList` schema on subpages
